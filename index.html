FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Web Craft do?

We design and build websites, and connect them to the automation a business needs — lead capture, follow-ups and internal workflows — as one system.

How does a project start?

Book a call or send an enquiry. We discuss your business, goals, scope and the right approach before any project decision.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. Existing websites are reviewed first so we can identify what should be kept, improved, replaced or removed.

Do you provide SEO and maintenance?

Yes, both are part of the service model — SEO and performance setup are included from Starter, with ongoing maintenance available on every plan.

What is business automation?

Automating the repetitive parts of running a business online — enquiry routing, follow-ups and internal notifications — so leads don't sit unanswered.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Web Craft works with businesses in the US, UK, Australia and Canada.

How does the Free Website Audit work?

Enter a public website URL and we review the homepage's structure, SEO and performance signals — then walk through the results together.

How do payments work?

There is no online checkout. Pricing is agreed in conversation and handled directly between us.
