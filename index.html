FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Web Craft do?

We design and build websites, and connect them to the automation a business needs — lead capture, follow-ups and internal workflows — as one system.

How does a project start?

Send an enquiry and tell us what you are trying to build, improve or launch. We discuss your business, goals and scope before any project decision.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. Existing websites are reviewed first so we can identify what should be kept, improved, replaced or removed.

Do you provide SEO and maintenance?

Yes. SEO and performance foundations are included from Starter, with ongoing maintenance and support available on every plan.

What is business automation?

It means automating repetitive parts of the customer journey — enquiry routing, follow-ups and internal notifications — so leads do not simply sit unanswered.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Web Craft can work with businesses internationally.

How does the Free Website Audit work?

Enter a public website URL and the system reviews the homepage structure, SEO and available performance signals.

How do payments work?

There is no online checkout. Pricing is agreed directly in conversation and payment is handled after confirmation.
